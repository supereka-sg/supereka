Fire Protection Services

Fire protection system design and regulatory submissions for new and existing buildings in Singapore.

What we do

  • Fire protection system design to the Singapore Fire Code, coordinated with architecture and other building services
  • Fire safety plan submissions to SCDF through the CORENET e-Submission System
  • Support for the Fire Safety Certificate application after works are complete
  • Fire protection works for additions and alterations to existing buildings

Singapore regulations and standards

  • Fire safety plans are prepared and submitted to SCDF by a Qualified Person (a registered architect or professional engineer) on behalf of the building owner, through the CORENET e-Submission System. SCDF: Plan approval.
  • A Fire Safety Certificate is required before premises are occupied. The QP applies for it once the works are complete. SCDF: Fire Safety Certificate and Temporary Fire Permit.
  • Where a design departs from the Fire Code through an alternative solution, a fire safety engineer prepares it and a separate peer reviewer assesses it. SCDF: Plan approval.

Rules and standard editions change. We confirm the requirements that apply to your project at the start.

Frequently asked questions

Which authority approves fire protection systems in Singapore?

The Singapore Civil Defence Force (SCDF) reviews fire safety plans against the Fire Code. Plans are submitted by a Qualified Person through the CORENET e-Submission System.

What is a Fire Safety Certificate?

It is the SCDF certificate required before a building or premises can be occupied. The Qualified Person applies for it after the works are completed.

Can you upgrade fire protection in an older building?

Yes. We have worked on additions and alterations to existing and historic buildings, designing upgrades that meet current requirements while keeping the work as unobtrusive as possible.
