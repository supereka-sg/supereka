Air-Conditioning & Mechanical Ventilation Services (ACMV)

Design and supervision of air-conditioning and mechanical ventilation systems for offices, retail, F&B, central kitchens, homes and places of worship in Singapore.

What we do

  • Cooling load calculations and air-conditioning system selection
  • Ventilation and exhaust design, including kitchen exhaust for F&B outlets and central kitchens
  • Design to Singapore standards such as SS 553 and the requirements of the relevant authorities
  • Tender documents, contractor evaluation and site supervision
  • Additional air-conditioning systems for existing premises

Where we have applied it

SUPEREKA has delivered building services for CHAGEE outlets, central kitchens, HDB neighbourhood upgrading, places of worship, offices and landed homes. See the full project list.

Frequently asked questions

What does ACMV stand for?

ACMV stands for air-conditioning and mechanical ventilation. It covers the systems that cool, ventilate and exhaust air in a building.

Do you design ventilation for F&B outlets and central kitchens?

Yes. SUPEREKA has provided building services design for eleven central kitchen developments and over twenty F&B outlets in Singapore, including kitchen exhaust and fresh-air provision.

Can an air-conditioning system be added to an existing building?

Yes. We have designed additional air-conditioning systems for existing premises, and we plan the work to minimise disruption to the building and its occupants.
