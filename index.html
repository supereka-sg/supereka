About Us.

Established in 2018, SUPEREKA is a building services consultancy in Singapore, founded by a collective of architects and mechanical and electrical engineers. We provide building owners, developers and builders with air-conditioning and mechanical ventilation (ACMV), electrical and fire protection design, Qualified Professional endorsement, project management and due diligence.

Our work spans 55+ projects: 19 CHAGEE outlets, 11 central kitchens, HDB neighbourhood upgrading, places of worship, commercial buildings and landed homes.

Why choose SUPEREKA?

Multi-disciplinary expertise: architects and M&E engineers working together, so systems integrate cleanly with the building.

Collaboration: we work closely with clients, fellow consultants and builders to deliver quality projects through to completion.

A diverse clientele: private developers, public organisations, builders and individual property owners, across projects of every scale.

Practical innovation: energy-efficient, maintainable solutions that suit the way each building is actually used.
